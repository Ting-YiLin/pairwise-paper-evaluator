# Limitations

- There is no human-expert ground truth in the current release.
- AI reviewers can share correlated errors, even when they are run separately.
- Near-tie judgments may depend on evaluator-family weights, and different model families can disagree.
- The recorded position-reversal audits were not probability-sampled.
- A local comparison matrix depends on its reference set and grouping; its order is not a universal latent-quality order.
- Evidence cards can transmit an initial reader's framing.
- Front-door impressions from title, abstract, and introduction differ from full-text assessments.
- Manuscript files can expose author or journal identity through content or metadata. Keep source files local and remove identifying metadata where appropriate.
- Aggregate scores can exaggerate small verdict differences. Inspect edge-level evidence, confidence, and ties.
- This workflow supports research decisions; it does not establish objective truth about paper quality.
