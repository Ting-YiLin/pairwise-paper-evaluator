# Validation evidence

## Current observed evidence

The frozen project records contain 14 position-reversal audit judgments, all directionally stable, with 0 observed flips. The audits were not probability-sampled; this count is a description of recorded cases, not an estimate of a general zero-bias rate. At least one boundary comparison produced a meaningful cross-model disagreement, so near-tie outcomes can depend on evaluator family.

These observations support a limited claim about practical internal stability in those recorded comparisons. They do not establish general reliability or validity.

## Evidence levels

- **Internal reliability:** repeated or reversed judgments under the same workflow. This is the main evidence represented by the current audit count.
- **Cross-method validity:** whether independent methods produce appropriately similar or complementary results. Not established here.
- **Cross-model validity:** whether different evaluator families agree. A recorded disagreement shows this remains an open issue.
- **External criterion validity:** agreement with human expert judgments or another defensible external criterion. No human-expert ground truth is available in this release.

## Reproducing the public audit summary

The public number was derived from a frozen private authority package. Private source files and benchmark materials are not included in this repository. The audit count is not a population estimate.
