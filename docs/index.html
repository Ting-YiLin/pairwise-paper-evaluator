Why use pairwise comparison?

Comparing two manuscripts at a time makes trade-offs easier to inspect and discuss. Evidence cards, explicit decision hinges, and source locators help show what drove a judgment and where authors might focus a revision.

How it works

01 · Prepare
Define the comparison purpose and freeze evidence cards for each manuscript.
02 · Compare
Record a structured judgment with confidence, a decision hinge, and evidence locations.
03 · Review
Validate results, inspect the edge-level record, and use selected position reversals as stability audits.

Repeated evaluations and position-reversal checks showed encouraging practical stability in development use.
